yield: "8 waffles"
servings: 4
calories:

ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1-1/2 cups milk
- 1/3 cup vegetable shortening
- 1/3 cup butter
- 2 large eggs, room temperature

directions:
- In a small bowl, mix together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
- In a medium bowl, add milk, shortening, and butter. If you do not have shortening, replace it with another 1/3 cup of butter (total 2/3 cup butter). Microwave until butter is nearly all melted.
- Stir in the flour mixture until well mixed.
- Add the eggs and mix. Do not over beat the eggs.
- Pour into your waffle maker!
